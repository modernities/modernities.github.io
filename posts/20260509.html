There's a bottle of mezcal in my freezer I bought for someone. Opening it would mean it is over. The apartment looks like every apartment. Same lamp, same chair, same white wall that catches light the same way. I chose all of it, slowly, over years.

I have a suspicion there's no layer underneath. The "real me" people keep insisting on is just the surface, fed back.

The freezer hums. The lamp is on a timer.
